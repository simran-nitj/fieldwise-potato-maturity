PROJECT NOTES

About Fieldwise

A local research prototype for observing potato growth stages from field images and organizing provisional harvest planning context.

OBJECTIVE

Make image-based observations easier to review.

Fieldwise runs the project's existing YOLOv8n checkpoint on uploaded potato crop images. For each image it saves an original and an annotated view, plus class counts and confidence scores. The model classes are Vegetative, Tuber Bulking, and Maturity.

The inference path accepts JPG, JPEG, and PNG images, validates them before model use, runs one image at a time, and keeps completed assessment records with their result files under the local instance storage.

MODEL CONTEXT

Detection is one source of evidence.

The model reports boxes and confidence values. Confidence describes how strongly the detector scores a box; it is not accuracy. Accuracy is evaluated by comparing model outputs against verified labels on held-out data.

HOW TO READ THE ESTIMATE

A visible stage summary plus an explicit calendar assumption.

01

Upload field photos

Choose up to 12 readable images. No location, variety, or crop duration is silently filled in.

02

Review detections

Counts and stage shares come from model boxes in the uploaded sample. They may not represent the whole field.

03

Plan provisionally

If you provide planting date, assumed crop duration, and detections, a transparent rule adjusts a calendar midpoint and margin.

Scientific limitation. YOLO does not predict days to harvest. The date range is a preliminary rule-based estimate. Verify crop readiness in the field and use agronomic judgment.
DATA AND STORAGE

Project data stays local.

Assessment records are stored as JSON with original and annotated images in instance/outputs/. The Agri-IoT workbook, when available, is displayed as historical context and is not a live sensor feed or linked to current field records.

Model training uses verified annotations. Model-generated pseudo-labels are not ground truth unless a person has reviewed and corrected them.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS

Use as a research prototype.

  • Small verified dataset and limited source capture groups.
  • Source plot groups overlap between current train, validation, and test subsets.
  • Images, lighting, cultivar, and field conditions can change detector behavior.
  • Harvest timing needs local agronomic validation.
